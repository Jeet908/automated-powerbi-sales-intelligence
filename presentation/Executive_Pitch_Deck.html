THE BUSINESS CRISIS & BLIND SPOTS

The Challenge of Growth Without Realized Profit

SLIDE 02 / 08
THE CORE PROBLEM

The Top-Line Illusion

While top-line gross sales expanded by +14% YoY, operating profit margins remained unexpectedly flat at 21.4%. Leadership lacked dimensional visibility into which SKUs and territories were actively diluting cash flow.

OPERATIONAL BOTTLENECKS
  • 48-Hour Reporting Latency: Finance spent 2 days every week manually cleaning disparate CSV spreadsheets.
  • Discretionary Discounting: Regional teams applied promotional discounts up to 20% to chase volume quotas.
  • The Aggregate Trap: Blended reporting masked unprofitable territories behind high-performing markets.
Business Risk: High volume subsidizing negative-margin order fulfillment Objective: Implement real-time automated KPI audit

ENTERPRISE DATA ARCHITECTURE

Star Schema Modeling & Automated ETL Pipeline

SLIDE 03 / 08

Relational Star Schema Model

  • Fact_Sales (25k rows): Primary transaction records tracking gross sales, net revenue, unit costs, and profit.
  • Dim_Date (1,096 days): Time dimension supporting fiscal quarters, months, and sequential sorting.
  • Dim_Product (11 SKUs): Standard costs, target margins, and category hierarchies.
  • Dim_Customer (500 entities): Segmented into Consumer, Corporate, and Small Business tiers.
  • Dim_Region (6 territories): Direct mapping of regional managers and geographic boundaries.
PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATION

Strict 1-to-Many Single Direction

Eliminated many-to-many ambiguity and bi-directional cross-filtering, ensuring lightning-fast in-memory DAX rendering.

Dim_Product [1] → [*] Fact_Sales [ProductKey]
Dim_Region [1] → [*] Fact_Sales [RegionKey]

THE DISCOVERY: THE MARGIN LEAKAGE TRAP

Quadrant Analysis: Margin % vs. Sales Volume

CASH COW (HIGH MARGIN / HIGH VOLUME)

Technology & Displays

  • Low discount elasticity; buyers prioritize performance over promotional coupons.
PROFIT BLEEDER (HIGH VOLUME / ERODED MARGIN)

GEOSPATIAL & CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION

Territory Disparity & High-Yield Personas

SLIDE 06 / 08

Regional Territory Performance

  • US-West & Central Europe: Powerhouse regions driving 42.4% of total profit with consistent 26%+ margins.
  • UK & Ireland: Experienced a 4.1% margin drag attributed to elevated customs, transit freight, and regional price discounting.
  • South Asia (India): Fastest-growing territory (+24% YoY volume) with rapid corporate B2B expansion.

Customer Tier Economics

  • Corporate B2B (30% of accounts): Highest ticket size ($340 AOV) and lowest discount sensitivity. Primary profit stabilizer.
  • Small Business (15%): High loyalty, consistent quarterly replenishment cadences.
  • Direct Consumers (55%): High volume, but accounted for 78% of all discounted transactions.
